Privacy & cookies

This page describes what the Codevalanche product website stores and what optional analytics does.

Essential storage

The site stores a first-party cookie named codevalanche_consent to remember whether you accepted or declined optional analytics, and a browser localStorage entry named codevalanche_theme to remember your light or dark theme choice. Neither leaves your browser. It is used only for that preference and expires after one year.

Optional analytics

If you choose Accept analytics, the site may load Google Analytics 4 using the measurement ID configured for this website. It is used for aggregate page views and basic interactions such as section navigation and feature selection. It is not used to receive source code, prompts, form data, account information, query-string contents, or browsing history from this site.

If the website has not been configured with a real measurement ID, no analytics provider is loaded even after acceptance.

Your choice

You can decline analytics, or change your preference at any time using Manage cookies in the footer. Blocking or deleting cookies in your browser also removes the stored preference.
